---
description: One job's whole story on one card: the money position, budget vs actual per line, variations, progress claims, the crew with their insurance state, the schedule and the site diary. Run it before any conversation about the job.
---

1. Run `npm run builders -- job <ref or name>`. Partial matches work ("101", "Donovan").
2. Read the whole card before answering anything. The lines that matter most:
   - **The money line**: claimed vs contract ceiling, what is OWED, and the forecast margin.
   - **Budget vs actual**: any line OVER or near. `budget <job>` is the same table standalone.
   - **Variations**: anything still proposed is unclaimable and should have no work happening.
   - **The crew**: any insurance state that is not "current" gets said out loud.
3. If the operator is about to ring the client, lead with: what is owed, what is waiting on their approval, and what happens next on site.
