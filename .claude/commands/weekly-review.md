---
description: The Monday review, written from three commands. What needs a decision, the money position, the compliance state, then the five things that matter this week.
---

Run these three, in this order, and write the review from what they return. Do not write anything they do not support.

```
npm run builders -- attention
npm run builders -- profit
npm run builders -- compliance
```

Then write it in this shape, no more than a page:

1. **The week in one line.** Active jobs, money owed, variations waiting on approval, quotes out and their value.
2. **The one that cannot wait.** Any overdue claim and any uninsured subbie on site, by name. This section existing at all is an emergency; empty is the system working.
3. **The money in.** Each unpaid claim with its days, and who gets rung today. Any job with work done and nothing claimed in 30 days.
4. **The money out.** Budget lines OVER or near, with the cause named. Open purchase orders past 30 days.
5. **The paperwork.** Unapproved variations with their value, unsigned contracts, retention due for release, insurance renewals due.
6. **The pipeline.** Quotes going cold, with the call that saves each.
7. **The five things to do this week.** Picked from the attention list, weighted by money owed first, uninsured risk second, margin third, and say why each made the list.
8. **One thing to decide.** The single item that needs a person, not a process.

Add `npm run view` if the operator wants pages for a meeting: same numbers, the business's brand, and they print.

Numbers come from the commands. If a number is not in the output, it does not go in the review.
