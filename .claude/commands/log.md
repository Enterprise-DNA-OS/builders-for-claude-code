---
description: The site diary. A call, a decision, a delay, a delivery, an instruction from the client, onto the record the moment it happens. In a dispute, the diary is the defence.
---

1. Run `npm run builders -- log <target> "what happened"`. The target is a job ref, a claim ref, a subbie or a client; partial matches work.
2. Write the entry the way it would be read out in a dispute two years from now: who said what, when, and what was agreed or instructed. Facts, names, dates. No editorialising.
3. Things that always get logged, no judgement call needed: a client instruction given on site, a payment promise, a delay and its cause, a subbie no-show, weather that stopped work, anything about a hold or a disagreement.
4. If the entry is really a variation ("can you also..."), say so and price it: `variation add`. A diary note is not approval to do the work.
