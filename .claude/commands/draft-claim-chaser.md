---
description: Draft the follow-up for an overdue progress claim, firm and factual, in the operator's voice. Drafts to drafts/, never sends.
---

1. Read the claim and the job first: `claims`, then `job <ref>`. Check the diary for promises already made (`log` entries mention them).
2. Draft to `drafts/` a short letter or email, in this shape:
   - The claim: number, amount, served date, due date, days overdue.
   - The contract position: claimed to date against the revised contract, so the number is clearly under the ceiling.
   - What was agreed since, if anything (from the diary), and that it has not happened.
   - The ask: payment by a named date.
   - One factual line on the Construction Contracts Act 2002: the claim is a payment claim under the Act, and if payment is not made and no payment schedule was provided, the amount is recoverable as a debt due. State it once, plainly, without threat theatre.
3. A person reads it, and a person sends it. Never send from here.
4. Log the draft: `log <PC-ref> "reminder drafted, due date given as ..."`.
